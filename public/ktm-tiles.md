# 1\. Company Overview

**Company Name:** KTM Tiles\
**Business Type:** Tile Showroom and Retailer\
**Industry:** Building Materials and Home Interiors\
**Founded:** 2019\
**Headquarters:** India\
**Business Model:** Retail, Wholesale, Project Sales, and Interior Solutions\
**Status:** Growing Regional Business

KTM Tiles is a tile showroom and retail business specializing in tiles, sanitaryware, bathroom products, flooring solutions, and related interior finishing materials.

The company serves individual homeowners, builders, contractors, architects, interior designers, commercial customers, and property developers.

KTM Tiles operates with a showroom-based retail model where customers can physically explore different tile designs, finishes, sizes, colors, and applications before placing an order.

The company also supports customers with:

- Product selection
- Design consultation
- Quantity estimation
- Quotations
- Bulk orders
- Project orders
- Delivery coordination
- Installation guidance
- After-sales support

# 2\. Company Story

KTM Tiles was started with the goal of making quality tiles and interior finishing products easier for customers to discover and purchase.

The founders noticed that customers often struggled to choose the right tile because there were many combinations of:

- Price
- Application

- Brand

A customer selecting tiles for a home may need different products for the:

- Living room
- Bedroom
- Kitchen
- Bathroom
- Balcony
- Outdoor area
- Parking area
- Wall surfaces

KTM Tiles was created to provide customers with a physical showroom where they could compare products and receive guidance before purchasing.

# 13\. Tile Quantity Calculation

Tile quantity depends on the area that needs to be covered.

For a rectangular floor:

```
Area = Length × Width
```

For example:

```
Room Length = 12 feet
Room Width  = 10 feet

Area = 12 × 10
Area = 120 square feet
```

Additional tiles are normally considered for cutting, breakage, pattern matching, and installation requirements.

The required additional percentage depends on the project and installation pattern.

KTM Tiles staff can help customers estimate the required quantity.
