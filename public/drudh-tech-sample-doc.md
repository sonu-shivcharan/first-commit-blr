# Drudh.tech — Company Profile

> **Note:** Drudh.tech is a fictional technology startup created for demonstration, documentation, RAG, and AI knowledge-base applications.

## 1\. Company Overview

**Company Name:** Drudh.tech\
**Company Type:** Technology Startup\
**Founded:** 2023\
**Headquarters:** India\
**Industry:** Software, Artificial Intelligence, Enterprise Technology\
**Status:** Early-stage technology startup

Drudh.tech is a technology startup founded by a group of college students who initially came together to build their final-year academic projects.

What started as a student project initiative gradually evolved into a technology company focused on solving practical, real-world problems.

The founding team noticed that many academic projects were built primarily to satisfy college requirements and were rarely used outside the classroom. Drudh.tech was created with a different goal:

> **Build technology that can move from a college project into a production environment and solve actual problems for real users.**

Today, Drudh.tech works across multiple technology domains, including:

- Enterprise applications
- Healthcare technology
- Agriculture technology
- Artificial intelligence
- Machine learning
- Business automation
- Data-driven applications
- Knowledge management systems

---

# 2\. Company Story

Drudh.tech started when a small group of students wanted to build a meaningful final-year project rather than a conventional academic prototype.

The initial project involved developing a software platform to solve a practical problem faced by organizations.

While working on the project, the team encountered challenges that were not fully covered by their academic curriculum, including:

- Software deployment
- Scalability
- Authentication
- Database management
- Security
- User experience
- API development
- Cloud infrastructure
- Monitoring
- Maintaining software after deployment

These challenges changed the team's perspective.

The students realized that building software for a college demonstration and building software for real users are very different activities.

After completing their academic projects, the team decided to continue developing their technology and explore real-world applications.

This became the foundation of Drudh.tech.

The company gradually expanded its focus from student projects to production-oriented software development.

# 5\. Core Philosophy

Drudh.tech follows a simple product philosophy:

```
Problem
   ↓
Understand
   ↓
Build
   ↓
Validate
   ↓
Deploy
   ↓
Improve
```

Before building a product, the team attempts to understand the actual problem and the people affected by it.

The company considers questions such as:

1. Who has the problem?
2. How is the problem currently solved?
3. What makes the current solution inefficient?
4. Can technology meaningfully improve the situation?
5. What is the smallest useful solution?
6. How can the solution be deployed in a real environment?
7. How can its impact be measured?

# 7\. Product Development Approach

Drudh.tech follows an iterative product development process.

## Step 1: Problem Discovery

The team first identifies a real problem through discussions with:

- Users
- Organizations
- Domain experts
- Business stakeholders

## Step 2: Problem Validation

The team determines whether the problem is significant enough to justify building a solution.

## Step 3: Prototype

A small prototype or proof of concept is developed to validate the technical approach.

## Step 4: MVP

The minimum viable product is developed with the core functionality required by initial users.

## Step 5: Real-World Testing

The software is tested with real workflows and users.

## Step 6: Production Deployment

Once validated, the application can be deployed into a production environment.

## Step 7: Continuous Improvement

User feedback, analytics, operational issues, and changing requirements are used to improve the product.

# 9\. RAG and Knowledge Systems

Drudh.tech considers Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) an important technology for building enterprise knowledge systems.

A typical Drudh.tech RAG system can connect organizational knowledge sources such as:

- PDFs
- Internal documents
- Company policies
- Product documentation
- Knowledge bases
- Databases
- Support articles
- Research documents

The system retrieves relevant information before generating an answer.

A simplified architecture is:

```
User Query
    ↓
Query Processing
    ↓
Embedding Generation
    ↓
Vector Search
    ↓
Relevant Documents
    ↓
Context Construction
    ↓
Large Language Model
    ↓
Generated Response
```

The objective is to make AI systems more useful when answering questions about organization-specific information.

RAG can help reduce the need to retrain a language model every time an organization's internal information changes.
